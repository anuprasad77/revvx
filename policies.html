Exchange Policy

1. Exchange Window

We do not offer returns or refunds. Customers have 7 days from the date of delivery to request an exchange for their order.

2. Product Condition

To be eligible for an exchange, the product must be strictly unused, UNINSTALLED, and in its original packaging. Once a part has been installed on a motorcycle, it is strictly non-exchangeable.

3. Exchange Shipping Costs

The customer is responsible for paying all shipping costs when sending an item back for an exchange. Original shipping costs are non-refundable.
